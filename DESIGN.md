# Ayyappa companion design

## Direction

User-directed two-version guide, retaining the Ayyappa black-and-white identity: black section navigation, white reading pages, neutral gray states and rules, and original images in natural color. ABP Simplified process guide is the default; the version picker switches to the unchanged Nitya Puja PDF reader. The former mixed-source topics, chants, and checklists remain in a distinctly separate Companion area. ABP's nine original tabs have 54 individually selectable internal subsections; unrelated deity columns and prayer variants are never combined.

The version picker is visible above reading content, including on mobile and Companion pages, and hidden in focus reading. ABP's contents links to each tab's internal contents; a reader selects its subsection through direct links or the tab/subsection controls. Previous/Next moves within that tab. English prayer text is pronunciation, using supplied workbook Roman text when present and generated pronunciation otherwise; original English procedure instructions remain unchanged in either interface language. Direct extraction is not labeled OCR. Original spellings, source step-number gaps, and actual invocation counts remain visible. All PDF routes, pages, controls, and OCR warnings remain intact.

A shared Bhajans entry leads to a ruled, title-filterable list of all 252 contents entries. A title opens its complete original page range, with a title/page selector, Previous/Next, full-size zoom, focus reading, and a source-position marker on the first page. All eight contents pages can be read separately. The user selected preservation of the complete original pages, including source pronunciation, English meanings, and credits, instead of typed pronunciation-only extraction. Nothing is silently removed from those page images. Shared navigation also exposes the two dedicated Namavali readers; their English text is the original source pronunciation with diacritics, never a newly generated transliteration. A source-empty Telugu entry is an explicit gap, not an invented line.

## Tokens

`--black: #111111`; `--heading: #000000`; `--white: #ffffff`; `--ink: #202020`; `--muted: #595959`; `--line: #dedede`; `--canvas: #ffffff`. All interface colors, including focus, status, hover, checked, dialog, and mobile states, are achromatic. Status is also expressed through visible text, not color alone.

System typography: Segoe UI, Nirmala UI, system sans. The body reading size is adjustable from 16 to 24px; default 18px. Telugu has additional line height and more restrained heading scale.

## Composition and behavior

Desktop: sticky black chapter rail, search/language/share toolbar, a Telugu book-title introduction with its original color cover, direct Begin puja / Deeksha rules actions, and a ruled chapter index. Chapters follow the printed book's order, covering all 61 physical PDF pages exactly once across 15 divisions. The active page has a chapter selector, page selector, original/draft view selector, and sticky Previous/Next links that cross chapter boundaries correctly. Below 760px, the chapter rail becomes a horizontal strip; the cover becomes a compact adjacent image, controls wrap, and reading stays one column.

English reading defaults to pronunciation in English letters; Telugu reading defaults to original scans. The Read as selector also offers the original scan and draft Telugu text for comparison. Users can open any scan full-size and use native pinch zoom. Pronunciation and selectable text inherit unreviewed OCR errors, with a prominent warning that mantra spellings and two-column reading order can be wrong. Never style this draft as verified chanting text or an English translation. Search includes chapter labels and OCR drafts, with an explanation of recognition limitations. English titles and navigation stay English; pronunciation, not meaning translation, applies to prayers and book text.

Only the introductory daily topic uses the arch-cropped devotional photograph. Content sections are separated by whitespace and rules, not nested cards. Editorial/confirmation labels are visibly distinct from source citations. Source-library groups use native disclosure elements.

Language, topic, chapter, book page, section, and search are reflected in the URL. Topic, chapter, and page changes move keyboard focus to the reading area. Focus view works for the book and typed chant readers. The skip link preserves the current route. Controls have visible keyboard focus, minimum touch sizes, reduced-motion handling, and print styles. Materials-preparation checkmarks last for the current visit and do not claim religious completion.

Chant & Sing adds large prayer text, numbered reading positions, full-list and single-entry modes, and a focus view without navigation distractions. English selection shows approximate Telugu pronunciation in English letters; Telugu selection preserves Telugu. No English meaning translations appear for prayers. Guide prayer quotations follow the same rule, while explanatory prose is unchanged. Long vowels use aa, ee, oo; the helper is explicitly an approximate aid needing pronunciation review. Tamil material remains in the linked originals until verified transcription is available. Controls advance text and must never suggest audio playback where no recording exists. Morning/evening quick access uses local time with a manual override. The daily puja checklist saves per local date on the device, separately from the visit-only materials checklists.

## Content integrity

Do not hide source disagreements or imply every source file was consolidated. Preserve page/sheet references, the permanent Drive collection, and separate editorial health/safety guidance from source-derived observances.

Book-original content and other-source companion content must not be conflated. The chosen scan, including both padayatra pages, closing stotras, Harivarasanam, puja-materials pages, and bhajans, is available intact. Printed page numbers are physical PDF page + 2 after the cover; the cover has no printed label. English descriptions and chapter groupings are navigation/editorial aids, not source translations. Retain the source's historical customs as original text, not contemporary health or travel advice.
