# Ayyappa companion design

## Direction

User-directed two-source guide, retaining the Ayyappa black-and-white identity: black section navigation, white reading pages, neutral gray states and rules, and original images in natural color. The workbook is labeled **Source 1: Ayyappa Process Guide** and remains the default; the PDF is labeled **Source 2: Nitya Puja**. Internal route identifiers and original source content are unchanged. The former mixed-source topics, chants, and checklists remain in a distinctly separate Companion area. The workbook's nine original tabs have 54 individually selectable internal subsections; unrelated deity columns and prayer variants are never combined.

The version picker is visible above reading content, including on mobile and Companion pages, and hidden in focus reading. ABP contents and tab pages now read as numbered parent sections with visible, indented children. All procedure steps and prayers of up to 30 source lines are readable without another click; longer lists expose three-line previews and complete-reader links. Nine jump links support the longer ABP contents page. Subsection URLs and the tab/subsection reader controls remain available; Previous/Next moves within the selected tab. English prayer text is pronunciation, using supplied workbook Roman text when present and generated pronunciation otherwise; original English procedure instructions remain unchanged in either interface language. Direct extraction is not labeled OCR. Original spellings, source step-number gaps, and actual invocation counts remain visible. All PDF routes, pages, controls, and OCR warnings remain intact.

The Bhajans entry contains the 38 songs listed on Ayyappa Telugu's `bajanapatalu` page, in source order. Keep the pinned monochrome identity: strong title, one search field, and ruled rows with stable source numbering, original Telugu titles, and source-listed singer credits. Every row opens a detail view with those credits and a link to that song's source page. Keep third-party lyrics on the source website; do not copy them into the app. Search accepts Telugu title and singer text. Do not include the former curated 43-song list or its user-supplied excerpts here. Keep Source 1 prayers/stotras separately accessible.

The Bhajans destination contains only the 38 Ayyappa Telugu songs and Source 1 prayers/stotras. Exclude the former 43-song list and the separate confusing bhajan PDF archive, its extracted text, page scans, and links. Shared navigation also exposes the two dedicated Namavali readers; their English text is source pronunciation, never newly generated transliteration. A source-empty Telugu entry remains an explicit gap.

## Tokens

`--black: #111111`; `--heading: #000000`; `--white: #ffffff`; `--ink: #202020`; `--muted: #595959`; `--line: #dedede`; `--canvas: #ffffff`. All interface colors, including focus, status, hover, checked, dialog, and mobile states, are achromatic. Status is also expressed through visible text, not color alone.

System typography: Segoe UI, Nirmala UI, system sans. The body reading size is adjustable from 16 to 24px; default 18px. Telugu has additional line height and more restrained heading scale.

## Composition and behavior

Suprabhatam is a single continuous reader with opening prayer, 14 verses, and completion under internal headings. Its sidebar entry is a direct link, with no per-verse branch, selector, or Previous/Next stepper. Focus view remains available. Old subsection links open the whole reading and scroll to the requested heading; source data is not merged or rewritten.

Reading indexes share numbered headings and indented children instead of flat link rows or closed accordions. The Nitya Puja PDF contents exposes each printed-page link beneath its chapter. Chant & Sing shows short prayers in full and long lists as labeled three-entry previews. Companion topics show short guidance and preparation checklists directly; the long supplies table has a labeled child link. Existing topic readers use the same numbered headings and indented body. Numbering for source steps remains separate and unchanged.

Parent groups use 32px vertical spacing on desktop and 24px on phones; children indent 24px or 12px respectively. Inner groups use 24px spacing, and prayer lines use 8px padding with neutral separators. Reading text follows the adjustable font size. Long titles wrap, page links wrap onto new lines, and links retain 44px touch targets. Numbered lists retain list semantics when visual markers are replaced by heading numbers.

Desktop: sticky black navigation rail with semantic nested lists. Today & Telugu calendar is the first root. Both named source parents remain visible with indented tab/chapter children. Source groups, all workbook tabs, Bhajans/Namavali, and Companion groups have independent 44px disclosure buttons with aria-expanded/aria-controls and a rotating neutral chevron. Parent links remain separate from disclosure buttons, so expanding never navigates. Hidden lists leave keyboard navigation; the active destination's ancestors automatically expand when the destination changes. Manual expansion state lasts for this visit and survives language changes. A neutral one-pixel branch rule and stronger parent text communicate depth. Current-page marking belongs to the exact destination, not its ancestors. Below 760px, the same vertical tree occupies a bounded 320px scrolling region rather than a flat horizontal strip, keeping reading content reachable. Mobile selection scrolls only that region, not the document.

The passphrase-protected, read-only Swami Pooja Schedule is the first navigation destination, immediately before Today & Telugu calendar. A concise privacy explanation and password field lead to a plain semantic schedule table with the Swami name, address, date, weekday, period/time, host-selected puja, Evite, priest, and volunteers. Search filters in source order; on phones each cell becomes a labeled row while retaining table semantics. The snapshot is encrypted in public files, contains no source-sheet link, and is discarded from page state when locked, navigating away, or entering global search. Dates remain exactly as listed because the sheet provides no year.

The search/language/share toolbar, original color cover, direct Begin puja / Deeksha rules actions, and ruled chapter index remain intact. Chapters follow the printed book's order, covering all 61 physical PDF pages exactly once across 15 divisions. The active page has a chapter selector, page selector, original/draft view selector, and sticky Previous/Next links that cross chapter boundaries correctly. On phones, the cover becomes a compact adjacent image, controls wrap, and reading stays one column.

English reading defaults to pronunciation in English letters; Telugu reading defaults to original scans. The Read as selector also offers the original scan and draft Telugu text for comparison. Users can open any scan full-size and use native pinch zoom. Pronunciation and selectable text inherit unreviewed OCR errors, with a prominent warning that mantra spellings and two-column reading order can be wrong. Never style this draft as verified chanting text or an English translation. Search includes chapter labels and OCR drafts, with an explanation of recognition limitations. English titles and navigation stay English; pronunciation, not meaning translation, applies to prayers and book text.

Only the introductory daily topic uses the arch-cropped devotional photograph. Content sections are separated by whitespace and rules, not nested cards. Editorial/confirmation labels are visibly distinct from source citations. Source-library groups use native disclosure elements.

Language, topic, chapter, book page, section, and search are reflected in the URL. Topic, chapter, and page changes move keyboard focus to the reading area. Focus view works for the book and typed chant readers. The skip link preserves the current route. Controls have visible keyboard focus, minimum touch sizes, reduced-motion handling, and print styles. Materials-preparation checkmarks last for the current visit and do not claim religious completion.

Chant & Sing adds large prayer text, numbered reading positions, full-list and single-entry modes, and a focus view without navigation distractions. English selection shows approximate Telugu pronunciation in English letters; Telugu selection preserves Telugu. No English meaning translations appear for prayers. Guide prayer quotations follow the same rule, while explanatory prose is unchanged. Long vowels use aa, ee, oo; the helper is explicitly an approximate aid needing pronunciation review. Tamil material remains in the linked originals until verified transcription is available. Controls advance text and must never suggest audio playback where no recording exists. Morning/evening quick access uses local time with a manual override. The daily puja checklist saves per local date on the device, separately from the visit-only materials checklists.

## Content integrity

The **Today & Telugu calendar** surface is Read/Operate within the same monochrome world. A compact daily summary appears above guide content and disappears in focus view. The dedicated page has a prominent approximation disclosure, explicit location consent/city search, a date reading with semantic definition-list rows, named timing periods, a seven-column month table, and source links. At phone widths, labels stack above values and month cells wrap their short tithi labels; the calendar is not a separate colorful dashboard.

Austin, TX is the default location; a visitor's selected city or device location overrides it and remains saved only in that browser. Geolocation is never requested automatically, and location is not part of hash routes. Resetting the city returns to Austin. Source status differentiates fresh online solar updates, loading, calculated historical dates, and explicit online-failure fallback. Refresh occurs on selected-city midnight and every six hours while active, with foreground/reconnect checks. Approximate lunar values are computed locally using the bundled licensed engine and never presented as source-fetched Panchang data. The daily view starts at sunrise and marks transition times on the following civil date. The monthly view shows calculated sunrise tithis, not invented festival labels.

Shubha gadiyalu explicitly means daytime Choghadiya Amrit/Shubh/Labh after removing overlaps with the three daily avoidance periods, not Telugu Amrita Kalam or a guaranteed personal muhurtham. Source links request verification of the same place/date/timezone, and unsupported Varjyam, Durmuhurtam, and festival decisions remain external. No sunrise-based timing is invented for polar day/night.

Do not hide source disagreements or imply every source file was consolidated. Preserve page/sheet references, the permanent Drive collection, and separate editorial health/safety guidance from source-derived observances.

Book-original content and other-source companion content must not be conflated. The chosen scan, including both padayatra pages, closing stotras, Harivarasanam, puja-materials pages, and bhajans, is available intact. Printed page numbers are physical PDF page + 2 after the cover; the cover has no printed label. English descriptions and chapter groupings are navigation/editorial aids, not source translations. Retain the source's historical customs as original text, not contemporary health or travel advice.
